Privacy Policy

For ShiftAssist for UKG · Last updated: 26 September 2026
In one sentence: ShiftAssist reads the UKG Schedule Planner page you already have open, does all of its work inside your own browser, and sends no schedule or employee data to us or to anyone else.

1. Who we are

ShiftAssist for UKG is published by vibecode Lab, a sole proprietorship registered in Bangkok, Thailand. Contact: vibecodelab.dev@gmail.com.

2. What the extension reads

When you open the ShiftAssist panel on a UKG Dimensions Schedule Planner page, it reads the scheduling data already displayed in that page, which can include:

  • Employee names as shown in the planner
  • Shift dates, times, locations and job codes
  • Pay code entries such as leave, shift comments and availability

It reads this data only from the tab you are working in, only while you are using it, and only to perform the action you asked for.

3. Where that data goes

ProcessedEntirely inside your browser, on your own computer
Sent to vibecode LabNever. We operate no server that receives your data and we cannot see it.
Sent to third partiesNever. No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no error-reporting service.
Sold or sharedNever. We do not sell data, and there is no data to sell.
Leaves your computer only whenYou yourself copy a report or save a file, and choose where it goes

4. What is stored on your device

The extension stores a small amount of data locally, using the browser's own extension storage. It stays on your computer and is removed when you uninstall the extension.

Your settingsInterface language, panel position, audit rule thresholds, import preferences
Your working listThe shift list you paste in, so it is still there next time you open the panel
Last runA record of the most recent import so you can review what happened

5. Payment data

Purchases are handled by ExtensionPay, which uses Stripe as the payment processor. When you buy a licence, you give your email address and payment details directly to them, not to us.

  • We never receive, see or store your card number.
  • The extension asks ExtensionPay one question: whether this installation has a valid paid or trial licence. It receives yes or no, plus your licence dates.
  • We can see the email address associated with a purchase, so that we can provide support and issue refunds.

Their handling of your data is governed by their own policies: ExtensionPay and Stripe.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no schedule or employee data, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or delete on request. To erase everything the extension has stored locally, remove the extension from your browser. For anything connected to your purchase — a copy of it, correction, or deletion of your purchase record — email us and we will handle it within 30 days.

8. Children

ShiftAssist is a workplace tool sold to adults. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. If a change ever means the extension starts transmitting data that it previously kept local, we will say so clearly before it takes effect.
