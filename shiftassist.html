ShiftAssist for UKG

A paid Chrome extension for workforce planners who use UKG Dimensions Schedule Planner. It bulk-imports shifts, audits schedules against working-time rules, and exports reports — without leaving the Schedule Planner screen.

What it does

  • Bulk shift import. Paste a list of shifts from a spreadsheet and ShiftAssist enters them one slot at a time, checking each entry after it is written.
  • Location and job matching. Each shift carries both its location and its job, so a job code that exists in several departments lands in the right one.
  • Schedule audits. Checks the loaded schedule against working-time rules — consecutive working days, minimum rest days inside a rolling window, late-shift-followed-by-early-shift patterns — with every threshold configurable.
  • Report export. Exports schedules, shift comments and availability for the date range shown in the planner, as plain text or tab-separated files you can open in a spreadsheet.
  • Thai and English interface. Switchable at any time.
